GTXR / Fin design

Composite rocket fin development

The full design cycle for GTXR's composite fins: screening criteria, shape and core-material trades, alignment tooling, 3D-scan verification, and flight.

View case study→

Led the fin architecture trade. The goal was a design that could be built consistently across eight flight fins, not maximum single-fin performance.

  • Hexagonal cross-section, short taper: repeatable to machine with a higher predicted flutter margin, for a modest aerodynamic penalty
  • Full G10 core: accepted +1.24 lb for manufacturing confidence and repeatability
  • Led design of a modular four-tower alignment fixture; cant held within 0.1° of the 1.6° target on all four fins
73%

Less worst-case fin clocking error, measured by 3D scan: 1.041° before the fixture, 0.277° after, on the practice fin can, against a ±1° requirement.

Overnight passthrough recovery
